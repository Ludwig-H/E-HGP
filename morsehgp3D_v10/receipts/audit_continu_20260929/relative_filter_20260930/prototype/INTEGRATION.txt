Integration limits and next steps (audit prototype only)

Capabilities: fixed-size signed192 coefficients and positive192 denominator,
u32 coordinates, relative sphere preparation once, site intervals, relative
closed-box minimum-distance intervals, binary64 RN with explicit CPU guards.
Sphere holds private state; prepared results must not be forged or mutated.

For a future SiteTree port:
1. Prepare this relative descriptor once per query; no per-site integer192
   conversion and no absolute-coordinate centre in the double path.
2. An EXTERIOR site has a rigorous exclusion certificate; an INTERIOR site has
   a rigorous strict-interior certificate. AMBIGU must invoke the exact side
   predicate of the declared coordinate domain. This prototype has no such
   predicate, so AMBIGU is deliberately not a geometric answer.
3. A box is rejected only when its certified lower distance exceeds the
   certified upper radius. This prototype does NOT certify that every point
   of a box lies strictly inside; its box output is a MINIMUM distance.
4. Environment refusals require explicit propagation, not reinterpretation
   as interior, exterior, zero candidates or successful empty output.
5. A different preparation object is needed for arbitrary radius inputs,
   radius ordering, positivity of supports or ownership of q3/q4 events.
   Here R^2=||N/D||^2 relative to the anchor by construction.
6. A local extent fitting u18 does not justify old absolute floating shortcuts
   when global u32 offsets are large. Relative differences must precede floating
   conversion, and exact fallback still needs its own certified wider arithmetic.

No LiDAR/G4 benchmark, performance contract, nearest-neighbour index, census,
ball catalogue, tower or hierarchical clustering is acquired by these tests.
No claim that signed192 can encode the complete float32 geometric domain.
No transfer of CPU MXCSR qualification to GPU. CLI allocations are out of scope;
only the fixed-size filter operations avoid dynamic allocation.
