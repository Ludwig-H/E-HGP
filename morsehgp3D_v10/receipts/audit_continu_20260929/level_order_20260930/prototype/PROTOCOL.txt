Unsigned level comparator audit prototype, version1 (not engine/FULL)

Source level_comparator.cpp, C++20 with compiler unsigned128 support.
Internal arrays are LOW->HIGH; CLI inputs AND JSON products are HIGH->LOW.
All input limbs are decimal u64, no negative tokens; num uses5, den uses4 limbs.
One JSON answer per nonempty stdin line; no argv options.

C N_A4 N_A3 N_A2 N_A1 N_A0 D_A3 D_A2 D_A1 D_A0 N_B4 N_B3 N_B2 N_B1 N_B0 D_B3 D_B2 D_B1 D_B0
C compares N_A/D_A with N_B/D_B after guards on BOTH inputs:
0<=N<2^266, 0<D<2^200.
READY includes cmp(-1/0/1), lhs[8]=N_A*D_B, rhs[8]=N_B*D_A, each HIGH->LOW.
Refusals REFUSED_NUM_DOMAIN / REFUSED_DEN_DOMAIN / REFUSED_ZERO_DEN identify
the first invalid side A/B, in that validation order; neither product runs.
REFUSED_OVERFLOW means an unexpectedly nonzero ninth product word; no compare
of truncated products is allowed. It cannot occur on the guarded C domain.

M N4 N3 N2 N1 N0 D3 D2 D1 D0
M is a RAW checked multiplication primitive, NOT a rational comparison.
No266/200 restriction or denominator positivity applies; complete320/256
magnitude-bit inputs, including zero, are admitted for overflow tests only.
If product<2^512: READY, high_word0, product[8] HIGH->LOW.
Otherwise REFUSED_OVERFLOW and high_word>0 report the omitted ninth word.
Partial low words are deliberately NOT returned for normal overflow answers.
Malformed tokens, oversized decimal limbs or surplus fields: INVALID_INPUT.

Lemma: within the guarded C domain, each cross product is <2^(266+200)=2^466,
hence 8u64 words suffice. This lemma does NOT apply to unrestricted M inputs.
For the raw5x4 multiplication, each row sum a_i*b_j+word+carry<=2^128-1.
Earlier rows touch at most i+3, so row carry may be assigned to previously
untouched i+4; final row carry is the ninth word and is checked explicitly.
No Wide9 generic multiplication, silent resize or signed arithmetic is used.
Zero numerators are admitted. Exact primary arithmetic is integer-only and
has no FENV requirement. No allocation in arithmetic operations; CLI may allocate.

Mutations: MUTANT_FLOAT compares rounded double quotients (products still exact).
MUTANT_IGNORE_HIGH discards the raw ninth word; it is exercised by M overflow,
not by the guarded comparison, whose high word is mathematically always zero.
A mutant code0 is not a successful mathematical answer; independent Fraction
and integer judging must reject it causally.

No centre/support positivity, degree/radius construction, native ownership,
catalogue, FULL, GPU, timing or growth is implemented or qualified here.
smoke.cpp is only a tiny native non-vacuity gate, not an independent oracle.
Source/protocol are frozen before initial compilation; normal/UBSan and both
mutants will be logged separately. External system headers/libraries not bundled.
