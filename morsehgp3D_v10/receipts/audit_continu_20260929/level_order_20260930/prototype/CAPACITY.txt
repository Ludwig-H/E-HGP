Capacity proof and scope (audit-only)

Let B=2^64. Each loop accumulates a_i*b_j + old_word + carry.
Both old_word and carry are in [0,B-1], so the largest sum is
(B-1)^2 + 2(B-1) = B^2-1, representable in unsigned128 without wrap.
The row carry remains a u64. Before row i, earlier rows touched at most
word i+3, including their carries; therefore word i+4 is fresh and
assignment, rather than addition, is valid. In row4, i+4 is index8:
the entire ninth product word is recorded explicitly as high.

For C, N<2^266 and 0<D<2^200 on BOTH inputs before either product.
Each rational cross product is <2^466<2^512; high must be zero.
Eight product words are therefore sufficient only under these guards.
Comparing nonnegative cross products high-to-low is equivalent to
comparing the original fractions because both denominators are positive.

For M, operands can occupy all320/256bits. Product can occupy576bits;
a nonzero ninth word means no eight-word product may be accepted.
This primitive does not expand the accepted rational comparator domain.
M allows zero operands and has no rational denominator interpretation.
