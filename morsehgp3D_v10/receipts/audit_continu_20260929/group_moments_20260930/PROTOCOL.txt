Claim and assumptions
Every candidate sphere passes through an actual site a; its centre c belongs
to a closed box Q. Z is a subset of distinct actual site IDs, with their
actual integer multiplicities w_z, not freely chosen proof weights. With
W=sum(w_z), M=sum(w_z*z), V=sum(w_z*norm2(z)), define
S(c)=V-W*norm2(a)-2*(M-W*a).c. It equals the sum of weighted powers
F_z(c)=norm2(z-c)-norm2(a-c). Its exact maximum sigma on Q is at a corner.
The squared anchor radius is bounded by R2=max_{corner v of Q} norm2(a-v).
If R2>0, each F_z(c)>=-R2 and therefore S(c)>=-R2*p_Z(c), where p_Z
is the actual weighted STRICT-interior count. Thus, if sigma<0,
p_Z(c)>=ceil(-sigma/R2). To reject a candidate with admission threshold
theta, the division-free sufficient condition is sigma+theta*R2<0.
EQUALITY IS RETAINED. Contacts never contribute to p_Z. R2=0 is indecisive.
For current tight K5 catalogue leaves theta3=3, theta4=2. Weighted leaves
use the source's conservative theta=K-1; do not borrow tight thresholds.

Oracle independence and controls
Powers are evaluated directly as differences of Fraction squared distances;
the moment formula is independently cross-checked at all corners and at
fractional centres. The corner maximum of the affine sum and convex radius
is exact; finite centre sampling illustrates but does not prove universality.
The source checks positive barycentric support, exact shell/interior IDs,
all-corner individual dominance, grid/fractional samples, and exact power
events along the centre line. It checks a genuinely admitted K3 q2 example
with weighted interior mass 2 at equality sigma+2*R2=0; using <= is unsound.
It checks disjoint groups can be added. Two overlapping groups of credit2
each share one interior; blindly adding gives4 on an admitted tight K5 q3
with actual p=3. Safe max or moments of the ID union do not reject it.
Negative controls are Python logical mutations, NOT compiled engine mutants:
--mutant-equality and --mutant-overlap must fail causally with exit code1.

Proof and implementation boundary
The elementary quantitative implication is stronger than the historical
one-credit group-moment test, but no mathematical/literature novelty is
claimed. Anonymous credits are used only for safe whole-candidate rejection,
not seeded into an emitted I/U census. Groups may be summed only if their
actual site-ID populations are disjoint; otherwise use max, subtract shared
IDs, or recompute moments of the union. Artificial proof weights cannot be
interpreted as HGP multiplicity. Supports and contacts may belong to Z:
their power0 never counts as an interior.

For an implementation trial prepare a bounded small number of actual groups
once per local leaf or immutable global index node, then test each anchor
against these fixed groups in O(1) per group. Mark separately q3-dead and
q4-dead anchors before clique/support enumeration. The unresolved work is
sum_Q |anchors(Q)|*|tested_groups(Q)| plus group selection/preparation,
residual enumeration/census and actual output. Searching every global node
for every anchor or preparing groups per tuple can hide the original square.
No subquadratic global bound and no 100ms performance claim follows.
